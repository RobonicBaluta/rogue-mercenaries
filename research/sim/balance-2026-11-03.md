# Balance pass, 2026-11-03

The first balance pass over the skin builds (ROADMAP M8, "Balance pass with `rm_sim` at the end"), with the
simulator brought up to date first. The full generated report is `summary-2026-11-03.md`; this note is the reasoning.

## The simulator, before and after

Before this pass `rm_sim` played only each character's default skin, used a fixed 100 health, ignored healing items
and specials, and three of its checks were stale (they failed on every build since rewards became items and perks
moved to the menu). Now:

- Every selectable skin is played in turn (18 skins, 16 of them with a build; about 1780 runs each in the reference
  run below).
- Maximum health is the build's multiplier (and Excella's melee-kill health).
- Healing items from rewards, the shop, perks, chests (3 per arena, a key each unless the build is exempt) and enemy
  drops are used at low health through the core's own heal, so a build's heal multiplier and buffs apply.
- Grenades and mines are thrown as they come; a build that turns drops into its own item (Safari) gets that item
  instead of herbs.
- Specials are used whenever ready, with estimated effects (against Majini of 600 to 3000 health: the twin SMGs
  finish 1.2 enemies a use, the burst 0.6, a dash 0.35). Josh's backup partner, as seen in game, kills alongside the
  player (1.5x as fast), draws some of the enemies' attacks (0.8x) and revives a player bleeding out once per arena.
- Each run equips a random unlocked perk per PERKS slot; the profile buys the cheapest unlock after each run.
- Fixed: reward offers compared without their item, the run map's top tier (one tier by design) counted as a bad
  route choice, menu perks and build starting weapons counted as unreachable, and the arenas histogram stopped at
  three, merging runs that cleared every arena with runs that died in the last. All invariants pass again; x64 and
  Win32 give identical results.

New option `--hit-scale X`: enemies hit X times as often. The model's own constants predate healing items and chests
(with them, 97 percent of runs complete at 1.0); 2.5 puts the overall completion near half, where differences
between builds show. It calibrates the model, not the game: the numbers below compare builds with each other and are not
predictions of how often players finish.

Spread across builds: 4 to 98 percent (standard deviation 28.7 points) before, 19 to 93 percent (23.6) after;
overall completion 54.2 then 55.4 percent.

What drives the model: healing. A heal on every kill (Sheva's and Rebecca's traits) restored most of a health bar per
arena (an arena takes 20 to 38 kills), and the traits that only add damage (Jill's crit) or supplies (Josh, Excella) gave
little survival. Midnight's half health had nothing to offset it, Locksmith's key exemption rarely mattered (keys drop
at 15 percent per Majini kill, so the others miss about two chests a run), and Safari's drops all become mines, so it
never finds a herb.

## Changes (configs, written by `scripts/write_perks.py`)

| What | Before | After | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sheva's Field Training (heal on kill) | 3 percent | 2 percent | the strongest trait by far |
| Rebecca's Combat Medic (heal on kill) | 4 percent | 2 percent | the same, on top of her spray per clear |
| Field Surgeon heal multiplier (Rebecca) | 1.5 | 1.3 | still the strongest build |
| Jill's Battle Instinct (crit chance) | 10 percent | 15 percent | damage traits gave little; Jill BSAA was near the bottom |
| Tribal Sheva's Quiver | arrows only | arrows, and 1.5 percent heal on kill | the skin swaps out Sheva's own heal |
| Uroboros Speed (Midnight) health | 0.5x | 0.7x | the drawback stays, but half health was 4 percent |
| Locksmith (Jill BSAA) | no keys needed | no keys needed, 3 more chests per arena | its exemption alone rarely mattered (new build field `extra_chests`) |
| Mine Layer (Safari) health | 1.0x | 1.25x | no herbs ever (every drop is a mine): a tougher Chris instead |

Every map has at least 7 chest positions (most 8), so Locksmith's six fit everywhere. Seen in game on the mod's own
run (`build/runs/20261103-213304-locksmith/`): arena 1 (Ship Deck) and arena 2 (Prison) each placed 6 of 6 chests.
The other changes only change the numbers of effects the core already has (health multiplier, heal multiplier, heal
on kill, crit chance); no code changed for them.

Unchanged on purpose:

- **Ambition (Excella), Heavy Gunner (Heavy Metal), Call for Backup (Josh).** The model cannot judge them: Excella's
  stats come from melee kills (the model melees one hit in ten), Heavy Metal's strength is the Gatling and its ammo,
  and Josh's partner is an estimate. Their real strength needs playtests.
- **Field Kit (S.T.A.R.S. Chris), Iron Fist (S.T.A.R.S. Wesker), Twin Fury (Jill Battle Suit)** stay near the top,
  with Field Surgeon, because each one's place rests on something the model handles poorly: it never wastes a heal
  (Field Kit's spray per clear is used below 35 percent health, every time), it melees one hit in ten (Iron Fist is a
  melee build), and Twin Fury's 1.2 kills a use is an estimate. Lowering them on the model's word alone could weaken
  builds that play fine.

Next: playtest feedback first. The model ranks builds by survival, while in game killing fast also keeps enemies
off the player and the enemy mix changes by arena, so a build the user finds too strong or too weak in play outranks
this table.
