## Player model

Per arena: kill intervals ~ Exp(7 s / (mean clamped multiplier x skill)), skill ~ U(0.8, 1.3) per run; 3-6 hits per enemy at decreasing target HP; 10% melee hits; incoming hits Poisson(0.075/s x tier scale 1.0/1.3/1.6 / skill), 5-11 damage before modifiers; 10% partner kills (not counted), 3% duplicate kill reports; +30 HP between arenas (per 100 of maximum); heal requests applied immediately. Maximum health is 100 times the build's multiplier and its melee-kill health. Healing items from rewards, the shop and perks are used under 35% health (spray and mixed herb 100%, green herb 40%, red only with a green), through the core's heal (the build's heal multiplier and buffs). Specials are used whenever ready: a dash finishes 0.35 enemies, the burst 0.6, the twin SMGs 1.2 (estimates); the backup partner kills 1.5x as fast with you and leaves 0.8x the hits for the rest of its arena. The arena's 3 chests are reached after equal shares of its kills (each needs a key unless the build is exempt; the kills are of a key class); a kill drops a green herb 4% of the time, or for a build that turns drops into its own item that item 20% of the time (any drop). Grenades and mines are thrown as they come, one every 6 s (a mine finishes 1 enemies, a hand grenade 0.8, an incendiary 0.6, a flash 0.3); the backup partner revives a player bleeding out once per arena, at 25% health. Each run equips a random unlocked perk per category (the PERKS menu); the profile buys the cheapest affordable unlock after each run.
