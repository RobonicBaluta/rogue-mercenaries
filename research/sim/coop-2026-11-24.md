# Local co-op in the simulator (2026-11-24)

The user's item 10 ("Finishing co-op"; the board: "co-op balance in the simulator"). `rm_sim
--players 2` plays every run with two players, the core's seats (`AddSeat`), each with their own
build, perks, health, items, reward and shop turn; the route is settled with the last player's
seat active, as the adapter's vote does. This is a balance model, not evidence of play.

## The co-op model (estimates, like the solo one)

- Both players kill at once: their kill rates (power x skill, the backup partner's speed) add up.
- The enemies alive at once (economy `coop.alive_mult`) split their hits between the players
  standing: each takes `alive_mult / standing` of the solo pressure.
- The arena has `coop.enemy_mult` times the enemies (the core scales the budget).
- A player at zero health while the other stands is helped up half the time (the game's dying
  state; back at 25 percent), else down until the next arena, which brings them back at
  `coop.revive_health` (the core). The run fails when both are down (the core).
- Boss health (`coop.boss_hp_mult`) is not modelled: an arena is its kills.

## Results (3000 runs each, seed 7, shipped configs)

| Length | Solo: complete, arenas | Co-op: complete, arenas | Co-op falls per run |
|---|---|---|---|
| short (4) | 96.3 %, 3.94 | 100.0 %, 4.00 | 0.004 |
| long (8) | 75.3 %, 7.31 | 99.8 %, 7.99 | 0.05 |
| marathon (12) | 42.3 %, 9.48 | 94.1 %, 11.87 | 0.42 |

In the model co-op is far easier: two players halve each one's pressure (1.3 times the enemies at
once, split two ways) while 1.5 times the enemies are cleared at twice the speed.

## What would bring co-op to solo's level (1500 runs each, seed 11)

| enemy_mult | alive_mult | long | marathon |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.5 (shipped) | 1.3 (shipped) | 99.7 % | 95.1 % |
| 2.0 | 1.3 | 98.8 % | 86.7 % |
| 1.5 | 1.8 | 95.3 % | 70.9 % |
| 2.0 | 1.8 | 90.6 % | 54.2 % |
| 2.5 | 1.8 | 83.6 % | 42.4 % |
| 3.0 | 1.8 | 76.3 % | 31.5 % |

The enemies at once (`alive_mult`) matter most. Solo's numbers (75 % long, 42 % marathon) come
back at about 2.5 times the enemies, 1.8 times as many at once. Not changed: the user's
two-controller sessions set the feel ("up a bit the amount of enemies at 1 time", 2026-11-21:
1.3), and the model's partner help and split are guesses. A choice for the user.
